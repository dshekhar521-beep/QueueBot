# QueueBot

### Smart Customer Support & Ticket Management System

QueueBot is a Java and Spring Boot based customer support and ticket management platform designed to help organizations manage customer issues, support tickets, products, orders, and customer interactions from a centralized system.

The system combines conventional ticket-management workflows with an intelligent ticket analysis module that evaluates incoming support requests based on category, sentiment, urgency, escalation risk, and priority.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer support teams often receive a large number of tickets with different levels of urgency and business impact. Manually reviewing and prioritizing every ticket can make it difficult to identify critical issues quickly.

QueueBot addresses this by providing:

- Centralized ticket management
- Intelligent ticket analysis
- Dynamic ticket prioritization
- Customer and seller management
- Product and order management
- Role-based authentication and authorization
- Ticket attachments and evidence
- Customer feedback management
- Operational dashboard and statistics

The goal is to help support teams identify important tickets quickly while maintaining a complete workflow for customer support operations.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Ticket Management

QueueBot provides a complete ticket management workflow.

Features include:

- Create support tickets
- View tickets
- Update ticket information
- Search and filter tickets
- Track ticket status
- Manage ticket priority
- Manage ticket attachments
- Store ticket evidence
- Associate tickets with customers and sellers

---

### 2. Ticket Intelligence

The Ticket Intelligence module analyzes support tickets and generates useful insights from the ticket content.

It evaluates:

- **Category**
- **Sentiment**
- **Urgency**
- **Escalation Risk**
- **Priority Score**
- **Final Priority**
